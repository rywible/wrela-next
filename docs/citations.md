# Citations

Every outside work that the toolchain uses: what it is, its rights, and how and why we use it.
If a line here is wrong, fix it in the same change that makes it wrong. The toolchain was lifted
from `rywible/wrela-compiler` at 0.33.0 on 2026-11-15; its record before the split on 2026-10-21
is in `rywible/wrela`'s `docs/process.md`.

Claude and Codex agents write almost all of the code, tests and docs; Ryan decides the goals and
the architecture, and merges every change. No generative image, sound, music, voice or video
model makes anything here.

## Fixtures

The text's font (`lib/frame/text.wrela`: capitals, digits, a few marks, each glyph strokes of
segments and arcs) is drawn for this repository by Claude
(`wrela-notes/next/scratch/ink/scripts/font.py` writes it); no font's outlines or metrics were
copied.

The rates fixtures in `toolchain/tests/rates` (docs/rates.md) are written by Claude for this
repository, each after the game's code it stands for: `sum`'s sum takes its terms' shape from the
JONSWAP spectrum and cos²ˢ spread as `lib/sea` uses them (above), with made-up numbers; `look`'s
value noise is the usual lattice of hashed values blended by a smoothstep, its hash's multipliers
made up; `table`'s 57 joints are made-up points.

The image fixtures in `toolchain/tests/images` are four flat colours that Claude made. The PNG and
zlib fixtures in `toolchain/tests/png` are generated patterns that Claude made with Python's
`zlib` and Pillow (HPND), and Pillow's decoder checked the 8-bit ones.
